About AgriCalc USA

Last updated October 22, 2026

AgriCalc USA is an independent educational website that gives American farmers, ranchers and gardeners free calculators and plain-English guides. Our goal is simple: make the everyday math of farming fast and transparent, and explain the reasoning behind it.

What we publish

  • Calculators for seed rate, fertilizer, irrigation water, grain storage, sprayer calibration, growing degree days, profit and unit conversion. Each tool shows its formula.
  • Guides that explain concepts with worked examples and point to official USDA and land-grant university resources.

How we work

We write in U.S. units, check formulas against standard references, and link to primary sources such as USDA agencies. Read our editorial policy for details on sourcing, updates and corrections.

What we are not

We are not a certified crop advisor, lawyer or financial planner. Local soils, weather, labels and regulations vary, so use our content as a starting point and confirm decisions with your state Extension office or a qualified professional. See the disclaimer.

How we are funded

This site is supported by advertising, including Google AdSense. Ads never decide what we write. Read the privacy policy to learn how ads and cookies work.

Contact

Questions, corrections or suggestions for new tools? Visit the contact page.
