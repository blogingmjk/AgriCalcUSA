How this calculator works

Land is bought, rented and reported in different units. Deeds and USDA programs use acres, engineering plans use square feet, and international buyers and research papers use hectares. This tool converts any of the six common units to all the others at once.

1 acre = 43,560 sq ft = 4,840 sq yd = 4,046.86 sq m = 0.4047 ha
1 section (1 sq mile) = 640 acres

Frequently asked questions

How many acres is a section?

A standard U.S. survey section is one square mile, which is 640 acres.

How many square feet are in an acre?

An acre is 43,560 square feet. A square lot about 208.7 feet on each side is one acre.

How do I convert acres to hectares?

Multiply acres by 0.4047. For example, 100 acres is about 40.47 hectares.
