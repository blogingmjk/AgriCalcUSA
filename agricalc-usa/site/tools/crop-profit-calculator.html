How this calculator works

Build a quick budget for any grain crop. Enter expected yield and price, then your costs per acre. The calculator shows revenue, total cost, net return, break-even price and break-even yield. The default numbers are illustrations only; replace them with your own records.

Net per acre = (Yield × Price) − Total cost per acre
Break-even price = Total cost ÷ Yield
Break-even yield = Total cost ÷ Price

Frequently asked questions

What is a break-even price?

It is the price per bushel you need to cover all listed costs at your expected yield.

Should I include land rent?

Yes, if you rent or want to include a charge for owned land. Otherwise your result shows return to land and management.

Where can I find cost estimates?

USDA ERS Commodity Costs and Returns and your state extension enterprise budgets are good checks.
