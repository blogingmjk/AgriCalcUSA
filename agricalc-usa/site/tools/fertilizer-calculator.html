How this calculator works

Enter the nutrient amounts recommended by your soil test or extension service and pick the products you plan to use. The calculator converts nutrient pounds into pounds of product per acre, credits the nitrogen contained in DAP or MAP, and totals tons and cost for your acres.

Product lb/ac = Nutrient lb/ac ÷ (Grade % ÷ 100)
N credit from DAP/MAP = Product lb × (N % ÷ 100)

Frequently asked questions

Does DAP count toward my nitrogen?

Yes. DAP is 18% N and MAP is 11% N. This calculator subtracts that nitrogen from your N target before sizing the N product.

What is the difference between P and P2O5?

Labels and recommendations use P2O5. Elemental P is about 43.6% of P2O5.

Is this a fertilizer recommendation?

No. It converts targets into product. Set targets from soil tests and local extension guidance.
