How this calculator works

Paste one line per day with the high and low temperature in °F. The calculator applies your base temperature and upper limit, then shows each day's GDD and the running total. Compare the total with your hybrid's GDD rating to plan maturity.

GDD = (High + Low) ÷ 2 − Base
86/50 method: cap highs at 86°F, raise lows below 50°F to 50°F

Frequently asked questions

What is the 86/50 method?

It caps daily highs at 86°F and raises lows below 50°F to 50°F, then subtracts a 50°F base.

Where do I get daily temperatures?

Use your local weather station, a state climate office or a university ag weather network.

Can GDD be negative?

No. Days that fall below the base count as zero.
