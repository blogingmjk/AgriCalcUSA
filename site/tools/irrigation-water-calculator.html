How this calculator works

Enter how much water your crop needs, your irrigated area and the efficiency of your system. You get the gross depth you must pump, total water in acre-inches, acre-feet and gallons, plus the pumping time at your flow rate.

Gross depth = Net depth ÷ Efficiency
Gallons = Acres × Gross depth (in) × 27,154
1 acre-foot = 325,851 gallons

Frequently asked questions

How many gallons are in an acre-inch?

One acre-inch is 27,154 gallons.

What efficiency should I use?

Well-managed center pivots are often roughly 75 to 90 percent. Use your system's tested value when available.

How much water does my crop need?

That depends on crop, growth stage, weather and soil. Use ET data from your state extension service.
