How this calculator works

Calibration confirms how many gallons per acre your sprayer really applies. Enter the nozzle flow from your catch test, your measured travel speed and the nozzle spacing. The tool returns GPA, acres covered per tank and the product to add to each tank.

GPA = (GPM × 5,940) ÷ (MPH × Nozzle spacing in inches)
Acres per tank = Tank gallons ÷ GPA

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure travel speed?

Time a known distance, such as 200 feet, at spray speed. Speed in mph = feet ÷ seconds × 0.682.

What flow rate should I enter?

Use the measured output per nozzle in gallons per minute from a one-minute catch test.

Can I use this for any product?

Yes for the math, but always follow the label for rate, volume and nozzle type.
